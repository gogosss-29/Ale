```
A hyper-realistic 9:16 iPhone front-camera selfie video using the exact same woman and product from the reference images.

The woman and the product must remain identical:
- same face, skin, hair, outfit
- same product packaging, label, color, shape
- no redesign, no changes

---

START FRAME

Matches the reference image:
- woman holding the product close to her face
- relaxed pose, natural expression

---

CRITICAL ACTION (VERY IMPORTANT)

In the FIRST 1–2 seconds:

- she quickly and naturally gives a small kiss to the product (playful, subtle, not exaggerated)
- immediately after, she brings the product slightly closer toward the camera

The movement must feel:
- spontaneous
- casual
- like a real moment, not acting

---

MOVEMENT FLOW

- kiss → slight smile → push product closer to camera
- product comes slightly forward but stays in frame (not blocking completely)
- small natural hand movement and micro-shake

---

TONE & EMOTION

- she loves the product
- expression: soft smile, confident, natural
- no overacting, no “ad energy”

---

BEHAVIOUR

- slight head tilt
- natural blinking
- relaxed posture
- subtle micro-expressions

---

CAMERA

- handheld iPhone selfie
- slight natural micro-shake
- imperfect framing
- no stabilization
- product briefly closer to lens during movement

---

LIGHTING

- soft natural indoor light
- realistic shadows
- no studio lighting

---

AUDIO

- no music
- optional: soft natural breath or subtle “mm” sound after kiss
- raw iPhone feel

---

STYLE RULES

- no cinematic look
- no filters
- no over-smoothing
```
